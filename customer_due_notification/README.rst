==========================
Customer Due Notification
==========================

This module adds a warning block at the top of the Sales Order form view when a customer has an outstanding overdue balance.

Features
========

* Displays an alert banner at the top of Sales Order form if customer has overdue invoices.
* Restricts visibility of the exact overdue amount to users who belong to the **Overdue Amount Viewer** group.
* Displays a generic warning message for other users.
